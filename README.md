A commercial-grade, production-quality Restaurant Food Ordering & Delivery web platform crafted with modern culinary aesthetics, glassmorphism, responsive architecture, and complete interactive functionality.

## 🍽️ Key Features & Capabilities

### 1. Brand & Visual Identity
- **Palette**: Obsidian dark mode (`#090D16`), vibrant flame orange (`#FF5A1F`), emerald status highlights (`#10B981`), and golden amber rating accents (`#F59E0B`).
- **Typography**: Google Fonts `Outfit` (headings) and `Plus Jakarta Sans` (interface text).
- **Glassmorphism**: Translucent frosted panels with subtle borders, glowing highlights, and micro-animations.

### 2. Multi-Role Ecosystem Switcher
Easily switch between all 4 platform personas from the top bar:
- 👤 **Customer App**: Browse cuisines, discover restaurants, customize dishes, manage bag, checkout, and track delivery.
- 👨‍🍳 **Kitchen Partner Portal**: Real-time Kitchen Display System (KDS) showing incoming orders, ticket timers, and cook/ready controls.
- 🛵 **Delivery Rider Interface**: Mobile-friendly dispatch dashboard with route waypoints, GPS status transitions, and trip earnings.
- 📊 **Executive Admin Portal**: High-level business analytics, weekly GMV charts, category market share, and order lifecycle management.

### 3. Customer Experience
- **Interactive Hero**: Location selector, live search, and animated floating food badges.
- **Horizontal Category Slider**: 10 curated culinary categories with icon hover glow.
- **Restaurant Discovery**: Filter by 4.5+ Rating, <30 min Delivery, Free Delivery, and sort by Recommended, Fastest, or Lowest Fee.
- **Restaurant Menu View**: High-resolution cover banners, ratings, cuisine tags, and category tabs.
- **Dish Customization Modal**: Real-time price calculation based on selected sizes (Regular / Large / Monster) and extra gourmet toppings.
- **Slide-over Shopping Bag**: Quantity increment/decrement, coupon validator (`FEAST20`, `FREEDEL`), tax/delivery calculation.
- **4-Step Checkout**: Delivery address, express delivery priority selection, safe payment methods (Card, Apple Pay, Cash), and order placement.
- **Live Order Tracking**: 5-step status progression timeline with animated courier motorcycle on an SVG vector route map, courier profile, and simulation controls.
- **Customer Dashboard**: Overview metrics, full order history with one-click **Reorder**, saved favorites, addresses, and profile settings.
- **Global Search**: Debounced live search across both restaurants and dishes with highlighted results.
